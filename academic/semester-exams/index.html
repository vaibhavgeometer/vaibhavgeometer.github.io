📖 Semester V Course Matrix (Current)

Total 20 Credits across 6 courses (5 Theory + 1 Practical). University Evaluation: 25 Internal + 75 External per theory course.

20 Credits Total
📐

Mathematics Core Courses (10 Credits)

MAT 301F Core · 4 Credits
Ring Theory and Linear Algebra

Advanced abstract algebra covering rings, integral domains, ideals, homomorphisms, quotient rings, followed by vector spaces, linear transformations, rank-nullity theorem, dual spaces, and quadratic forms.

Theory (L-T-P: 4-0-0) 25 Internal + 75 External
📥 MAT301F MCQs (UPH) PDF (~5.6 MB)
MAT 302F Core · 3 Credits
Tensor Analysis

Curvilinear coordinates, transformation of coordinates, covariant and contravariant tensors, metric tensors, Christoffel symbols, covariant differentiation, and Riemann-Christoffel curvature tensors.

Theory (L-T-P: 3-0-0) 25 Internal + 75 External
📄 View Syllabus Specification
MAT 303F Core · 3 Credits
Differential Geometry

Theory of space curves, arc length, Serret-Frenet formulas, curvature and torsion, helices, evolutes and involutes, first and second fundamental forms of surfaces, and geodesics.

Theory (L-T-P: 3-0-0) 25 Internal + 75 External
📥 MAT303F MCQs (UPH) PDF (~10.7 MB)

Physics Core Courses & Laboratory (10 Credits)

PHY 301F Theory · 4 Credits
Classical Mechanics & Statistical Mechanics

Lagrangian and Hamiltonian dynamics, canonical transformations, Poisson brackets, microcanonical/canonical ensembles, Maxwell-Boltzmann, Bose-Einstein, and Fermi-Dirac quantum statistics.

Theory (L-T-P: 4-0-0) 25 Internal + 75 External
📥 PYQ Paper 2024–25 (~4.1 MB)
📖 Classical Book 📖 Stat Book
PHY 302F Theory · 4 Credits
Quantum Mechanics & Spectroscopy

Wave mechanics, Schrödinger equation, 1D potential wells and harmonic oscillators, vector atom model, Zeeman effect, molecular vibrational-rotational spectra, and Raman spectroscopy.

Theory (L-T-P: 4-0-0) 25 Internal + 75 External
📥 PYQ Paper 2024–25 (~3.1 MB)
PHY 303F Practical · 2 Credits
Practical: Demonstrative Aspects of Optics & Lasers

Laboratory experimental study: Michelson interferometer, polarimeter, Babinet compensator, semiconductor laser diffraction experiments, and optical resolving power tests.

Practical Lab (0-0-4) Continuous Lab Evaluation
