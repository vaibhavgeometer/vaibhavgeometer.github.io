Piece Drops & Promotions • 9x9
In Design

Traditional Japanese chess with unique drop rules: captured pieces join your army and can be re-entered anywhere on the $9\times 9$ grid. Features kanji and internationalized directional piece sets with Fairy-Stockfish Shogi engine.
