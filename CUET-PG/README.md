## ⏱️ Recommended Strategy

1. **Chapter-by-Chapter (Practice Bank):**
   - Solve problems from DUET, BHU PET, and CUCET archives topic-wise right after completing theory.
   - Solve Section B (Domain Maths) from the 2022 and 2023 CUET papers.
2. **Untouchable Mock Bank (2024–2026):**
   - Save the 2024, 2025, and 2026 papers for timed mock tests in your final revision.
   - Set a strict timer for **90 minutes** for **75 questions** (~1.2 minutes per question).
   - Follow strict marking: **+4 for correct**, **–1 for incorrect**, **0 for unattempted**.
