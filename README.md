## 🌟 Key Features

### 🏛 1. 22-Year Official Mock Test Series (2005–2026 • 885 Official PYQs)
- **Complete Historical Coverage**: Full archive of official IIT JAM Mathematics examination papers from 2005 through 2026.
- **Two Distinct Exam Eras**:
  - **Modern & CBT Era (2015–2026)**: 60 questions per paper with authentic Section A (MCQ 1M & 2M), Section B (MSQ 2M), and Section C (NAT 1M & 2M) totalling 100 Marks (180 min).
  - **Classic Era (2005–2014)**: Objective papers (15 to 35 questions per year) reflecting the foundational era of IIT JAM.
- **Verified Master Answer Keys**: All questions are mapped to official IIT final answer keys and official numerical range acceptances.
- **High-Resolution Question Paper Screenshots**: Built-in 1-click modal to inspect original cropped paper screenshots (`assets/PYQs_Screenshots/`).

### 📥 2. 56 Pre-Compiled Publication-Quality Mock Test PDFs
- **Comprehensive Offline Test Archive**: 56 complete publication-quality LaTeX-formatted PDFs stored in `assets/Mock Tests_PDF/` and cataloged in `mock_tests_manifest.json`.
- **Each Mock Test PDF Includes**:
  1. **Cover & Structure Page**: Detailed examination statistics, pattern summary, time limits, and official exam instructions.
  2. **Question Cards**: High-resolution cropped question screenshots with metadata headers (Question Number, Section, Type, Marks, and Negative Penalties).
  3. **Official Master Answer Key Table**: Complete answer key and scoring evaluation rubrics on final page(s).
- **Direct 1-Click Downloads**: Available on every Year Mock Test card and Subtopic Mock Test card across the portal via `📥 Mock PDF` buttons.

### ⚡ 3. 27 Era-Based Subtopic Mock Tests (3 Eras × 9 Subtopics)
- Allows students to practice specific mathematical subtopics within distinct chronological eras:
  - **Recent CBT Era (2022–2026 • 9 Tests)**: 300 modern pattern questions.
  - **CBT Era (2015–2021 • 9 Tests)**: 420 standard CBT questions.
  - **Classic Era (2005–2014 • 9 Tests)**: 165 classic objective questions.
- Every test suite features interactive online CBT testing, untimed practice mode, direct `📥 Mock PDF` download, and topic-specific formula cheat sheet access.

### 🎯 4. 9 Comprehensive Full-Archive Subtopic Mock Tests (2005–2026 • 794 PYQs)
- **All-Time Topic Suites**: Aggregates all 22 years of questions for each of the 9 core syllabus subtopics into master tests.
- **Strict IIT JAM Syllabus Alignment**: Mapped directly to the official syllabus across:
  - Real Analysis
  - Multivariable Calculus & Ordinary Differential Equations (ODE)
  - Linear Algebra & Group Theory
- **Out-of-Syllabus Isolation**: 87 obsolete questions from topics removed prior to 2022 (e.g., Vector Calculus [Green's/Stokes'/Divergence theorems, surface integrals], Metric Spaces / Point Set Topology, Rings & Fields, Linear Programming, and Numerical Analysis) have been cleanly isolated to avoid distractive preparation for modern exams.

### 🛠️ 5. Custom Test & PDF Generator
- **Flexible Topic Combinations**: Select any combination of the 9 syllabus subtopics.
- **Customizable Question Count**: Choose between 1 and 100 questions with custom question type filtering (MCQ, MSQ, NAT) and era filtering.
- **Dual Launch Options**:
  - **Instant Browser PDF Compilation**: Generates and downloads a publication-quality LaTeX test PDF with answer keys directly in the browser.
  - **Interactive CBT Simulator**: Launches the custom generated test in the authentic exam interface.

### 💻 6. Authentic CBT Exam Simulator (`mock-test/test.html`)
- **Dual Examination Modes**:
  - **⚡ Official Exam Mode**: Strict countdown timer with visual urgency alerts (Green $\to$ Orange $\to$ Pulsing Red) and final submission modal.
  - **📖 Practice Mode**: Untimed exploration mode for self-paced learning and revision.
- **Interactive Question Palette**:

  - ⚪ **Not Visited**: Unseen questions.

  - Percentage accuracy, total time spent, and performance evaluation badges.
  - Interactive filterable question review (All, Correct, Incorrect, Unattempted).
  - Question-by-question solution review with official answer keys and explanations.
  - Best score tracking saved in browser `localStorage`.

### ♟️ 9. m,n,k-game & genralizations Analysis Board & SOTA Engine
- **Arbitrary (m,n,k) Configurable Geometry ($1\times 1$ to $32\times 32$, $k=1..8$)**:
  - Scales seamlessly from classic $3\times 3$ Tic-Tac-Toe, $4\times 4\times 4$ Qubic, and Connect Four to $15\times 15$ Gomoku and Beck's open $(\infty, \infty, k)$ problems.
  - Interactive board modal with sliders, cell count metrics, and theoretical classification displays.
- **Standardized [-100, +100] Win Probability Eval Bar**:
  - Calibrated win probability model: $+100$ = 100% win for First Player ($X$), $0$ = 50–50 balanced / theoretical draw, $-100$ = 100% win for Second Player ($O$).
  - Continuous vertical gauge with red 50–50 center line and board orientation flipping support.
- **Horizon-Free Static Board Evaluation (`evaluateBoardState`)**:
  - Solves $(m,n,k)$ horizon oscillations by evaluating center Chebyshev proximity and unblocked line segmentation on the static board, eliminating erratic score swings on theoretical draws like $(5,5,4)$.
- **Progressive AI Thinking & Iterative Deepening (Lichess Analysis Board Architecture)**:
  - In unsolved positions, the AI thinks progressively deeper ($d=1, 2, 3, \dots, 24$), refining move evaluations, re-ranking candidate lines, and discovering tactical Principal Variations as time elapses.
  - **AI Thinking Time Selector**: Quick pill selector offering `1s` (blitz), `5s` (standard analysis), `10s` (deep tactical search), `30s` (grandmaster ponder), and `∞` (unlimited until mathematically solved or stopped).
  - **Live Engine Telemetry & PV**: Real-time depth indicator (`depth 7/24` or `depth Solved`), evaluated nodes counter, computation speed in kN/s, live elapsed timer, animated progress bar, `Stop`/`Run` calculation toggle, and full algebraic Principal Variation sequence (`PV: c4 d4 b2 c2 b3`).
  - **Cooperative Asynchronous Non-blocking Architecture**: Micro-delays between ply searches keep the UI butter-smooth at 60fps with in-place board evaluation badge updates.
- **All-Square Evaluation Badges & MultiPV Candidate Ranking**:
  - Complete evaluation badges calculated across every single empty square for boards up to $10\times 10$, dynamically color-coded relative to the player to move (Best in green, Neutral in gray, Blunder in red).
  - MultiPV candidate moves list ranking top moves with real-time tactical annotations ("Instant Win", "Open 2 Build", "Positional Control", "Allows Opponent Win").
- **8 Calibrated Engine Difficulty Tiers (600–3000 Elo)**:
  - From Level 1 (Casual/Beginner) to Level 8 (StockTic Max) featuring Threat-Space Search (VCF/VCT), distance-2 neighborhood pruning, and retrograde tablebases.
- **Match Play Mode (`vs Engine`) & Evaluation Privacy Toggle**:
  - Play as X or O against any AI level with match evaluation toggle (turn evaluations OFF for realistic competitive tournament play, or ON for interactive learning).
- **FEN String & Game Notation Tools**:
  - Real-time FEN generation/parsing for $(m,n,k)$ positions, 1-click game record copying, and board state navigation.

Because the portal is built using standard web standards without heavy build tooling, you can run it locally with any simple HTTP server:

# Clone the repository
